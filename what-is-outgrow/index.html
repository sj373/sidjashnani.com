What is Outgrow? Alex Goldfayn's method for proactive, organic sales growth

Outgrow is a sales system created by Alex Goldfayn that helps companies grow their sales organically by getting everyone who talks to customers to communicate with them proactively for a few minutes every day, and by tracking those efforts every week. This page explains where Outgrow came from, the ideas at the heart of it, and how Sid Jashnani, CEO of Outgrow and Outgrow Certified Advisor, helps businesses put it to work. If you'd like to go straight to the source, Outgrow's own website is runoutgrow.com (opens in a new tab).

Who created Outgrow

Alex Goldfayn created Outgrow and founded the company behind it, Outgrow LLC. Outgrow's website describes him as the Founder and Creator of the Outgrow Sales Operating System. Alex has written several books on proactive selling, including 5-Minute Selling, Pick Up the Phone and Sell and Outgrow: How to Expand Market Share and Outsell Your Competition, which was released in April 2025 by Matt Holt Books, an imprint of BenBella Books. Outgrow is written for business owners and leaders and sets out the whole system in detail.

One point that often causes confusion online: Outgrow at runoutgrow.com has no connection with Outgrow.co, which is a quiz and survey tool.

The problem Outgrow sets out to solve

Alex's starting point is that most business-to-business companies are almost entirely reactive. Customers call their suppliers when there's a problem, and salespeople call customers when there's a problem, so the first thing a customer thinks when a salesperson rings is "What's wrong?" Teams become very good at solving problems and taking orders, but they create very little new business of their own. Because so few companies communicate any other way, Alex argues that it's easy to stand out simply by showing up and following up when nothing is wrong.

The core ideas behind Outgrow

The first idea is proactive selling itself, which Alex defines as "communicating with customers and prospects when they aren't expecting you (unscheduled), and when nothing is wrong."

The second is mindset. Alex teaches that behaviour follows thinking and that it's impossible for salespeople to outsell their mindset. Fear of rejection keeps many good people from picking up the phone, so Outgrow helps them see that they're helping their customers, not selling to them. A big part of this is letting salespeople hear, in their customers' own words, how much their help is valued. On its Sprint page, Outgrow sums up the mindsets it builds as COPE: confidence, optimism, positivity and enthusiasm.

The third is that everyone who faces customers can take part. Outgrow isn't limited to the sales team, and Alex includes inside sales, customer service and other customer-facing roles, because the more people making proactive efforts, the more opportunities a company uncovers.

The fourth is time. Alex designed the Outgrow communications to take seconds or a few minutes each, so they fit into a busy day. They include the "did you know" question, the reverse "what else" question, the pivot to the sale, the pivot to the next conversation, quote follow-ups, referral requests, and the voicemail followed by a text message.

The fifth is measurement. Outgrow tracks swings, not hits, meaning it counts the efforts people make rather than only the sales that result. In the book, Alex shares the success rates from Outgrow companies' logged actions. For example, about two in three voicemails followed by a text get a reply, and about one in four pivots to the sale gets a yes.

How Outgrow runs week to week

Outgrow runs on a simple weekly rhythm. At the start of the week, leaders ask for a certain number of proactive communications, often aimed at particular customers. During the week, the team makes them and logs them in a few minutes. Managers then share the numbers and the best wins with the team, usually in a short weekly huddle, so everyone can see what the effort is leading to.

What results Outgrow aims for

Outgrow's website says more than 500 companies have used the Outgrow process. The target for a company running Outgrow consistently is 15–30% organic revenue growth a year. That's the aim the work is designed around, and the weekly tracking shows whether a team is on course.

FAQs

Who created Outgrow?

Alex Goldfayn created the Outgrow method and founded the company, Outgrow LLC. His book Outgrow: How to Expand Market Share and Outsell Your Competition sets out the system in full.

Did Sid Jashnani found Outgrow?

No. Alex Goldfayn founded Outgrow. Sid Jashnani is CEO of Outgrow and Outgrow Certified Advisor, and he helps Alex execute the vision.

Is Outgrow the same as Outgrow.co?

No. Outgrow, the sales system at runoutgrow.com, is unrelated to Outgrow.co, which is a quiz and survey tool.

How much time does Outgrow take each day?

Alex built the Outgrow communications to take seconds or a few minutes each, so your customer-facing people can fit a handful of them into a normal working day.

What growth can a company expect from Outgrow?

Outgrow works towards a target of 15–30% organic revenue growth a year. It's a target rather than a promise, and progress depends on how consistently the team makes its proactive efforts, which is why those efforts are tracked every week.
