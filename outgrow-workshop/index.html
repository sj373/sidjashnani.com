The Outgrow Workshop: a full day of proactive selling for your sales managers and salespeople

The Outgrow Workshop is a full day with your sales managers and your salespeople, led by Sid Jashnani, CEO of Outgrow and Outgrow Certified Advisor. By the end of the day your team understands how Outgrow works from start to finish, has practised it on your real customers, and goes home knowing exactly what to do on Monday morning. Sid runs it in person across the UK, the US and India, or online if that suits your team better.

Why a whole day, and why together

Most sales teams have sat through a talk or a training session that felt useful in the room and was forgotten within a week. The Outgrow Workshop is built the other way round. The day spends less time on explaining ideas and more time on doing the work together, on accounts your people actually look after, so that they practise with their own customers while everyone is still in the room and can talk it through together.

Alex Goldfayn, who created Outgrow, describes a one-day launch workshop in his book as the way he teaches a team the Outgrow work, and the reasoning is simple. When a whole team learns the same approach on the same day, they come away sharing a language and an expectation, and that makes it far easier for managers to keep the habit going afterwards.

Who should be in the room

The workshop is designed for sales managers and salespeople. In Outgrow, Alex makes the case for including every customer-facing person who talks to customers regularly, which can mean inside sales, account managers and customer service people as well as the field team, because the more people making proactive efforts, the more opportunities you'll uncover.

Managers matter most of all. Sid has written in PREDICTABLE about Alex's view that working with managers has far more impact than working with the frontline alone, because a manager keeps the habit alive across a whole team every week, long after the workshop day itself. That's why the day gives managers a clear picture of their part in running Outgrow, as well as showing salespeople what to do on the phone.

What the day covers

The day starts with mindset, because behaviour follows thinking. Many capable salespeople hesitate to call a customer when there's no problem to solve, simply because they don't want to be a nuisance. Alex's answer is that you are helping, not selling, and once people take that on board, the calls start to feel natural and generous rather than pushy.

From there the team works through the Outgrow tools. These include the proactive phone call to a customer they haven't spoken to for a while, the "did you know" question that mentions something else you offer, the "what else are you working on" question that lets customers tell you what they need, following up on open quotes, asking happy customers for referrals, and the two pivots Alex teaches: the pivot to the sale, where you ask for the business, and the pivot to the next conversation, where you agree when you'll speak again. Each one takes seconds to ask, which is what makes them practical for busy people.

The team also looks at who to call. Alex's experience is that most sales teams spend the majority of their time with a small group of customers who ring them frequently, while a much larger group gets very little attention. Spending part of the day listing those quieter customers gives everyone a ready-made set of people to contact in the first week.

Practising on your real customers

Throughout the day, the team practises on real accounts rather than invented role plays. That's deliberate, because the hardest part of proactive selling is usually the first call, and it's much easier to make it with colleagues nearby and a manager who can help you think through what the customer said.

Leaving with a plan for Monday morning

Everyone leaves knowing what they'll do in their first week back, and managers leave knowing how to run a short weekly huddle where the team shares its numbers and its wins. In Outgrow, the team tracks swings, not hits, so people are recognised for making the effort, and the results follow from there.

In person or online, wherever your team is

FAQs

How long is the Outgrow Workshop?

It's a full day with your sales managers and salespeople.

Can the workshop be run online?

Yes. Sid runs it in person across the UK, the US and India, or online.

Who should attend?

The workshop is designed for sales managers and salespeople. If other people in your business talk to customers regularly, such as customer service or account management teams, it's worth mentioning them in your enquiry so Sid can talk it through with you.

Is this the same as the Proactive Sales Sprint?

Yes. The workshop used to be called The Proactive Sales Sprint, and the name changed to the Outgrow Workshop in September 2026. It's a separate thing from the Outgrow Sprint, which is an online programme run by Outgrow.

What happens after the workshop?

Your team starts its first week of proactive communications straight away, and managers run a weekly huddle to keep it going. If you'd like ongoing support, Outgrow Advisory continues the work week by week with your leadership team.

To ask about a workshop for your team, use the enquiry form on the homepage and choose "Workshop". Let Sid know roughly how many people would attend, where they're based and whether you'd prefer in person or online, and he'll reply with next steps.
