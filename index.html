About

Walked the path. Now I guide others through it.

Sid Jashnani is the CEO of Outgrow and a London-based Outgrow Certified Advisor. He helps leadership teams install proactive selling as a daily habit, aiming to compound it into 15–30% predictable sales growth, every year. Sales doing, not sales training.

Sid has been a founder since 2005. He scaled a systems integration business across the IT infrastructure ecosystem to £27M+ revenue. Along the way he lived the inconsistent quarters, the delegation gaps, the cash-flow pressure, and the daily firefighting most founders know too well.

Then he came across Outgrow, Alex Goldfayn's methodology. The insight was straightforward: operational excellence alone doesn't drive growth. Proactive communication does. Reaching out to customers and prospects when nothing is wrong. Sid installed the habit inside his own teams, watched it compound, and became an Outgrow Certified Advisor.

Today Sid is CEO of Outgrow, the company Alex Goldfayn founded, where he helps Alex execute the vision. His role is to bring clarity, consistency and commercial discipline to the business, and to support Outgrow's advisors and clients across the world. He also works directly with mid-market leadership teams across the US, UK and India.

Advisory

Outgrow Advisory: install the habit, track it, let it compound

A structured engagement to install the Outgrow system inside your business. Your team makes a handful of proactive communications a day. We track it weekly. For mid-market businesses generating $50M–$500M in annual revenue.

Diagnose the growth constraint

Identify whether the revenue gap is a behaviour problem (reactive selling, founder dependency) or a product-market fit issue.

Install proactive disciplines

Replace hope-based selling with daily proactive outreach to existing customers and warm relationships.

Create accountability cadences

Simple tracking and team rituals that make proactive selling a daily discipline, not an occasional effort.

Measure and compound

Track proactive activity and revenue outcomes weekly, so the habit builds year after year.

What you are working towards

  • Reach out to customers and prospects when nothing is wrong, as a daily habit
  • Remove the revenue dependency on the founder or any one seller
  • A target of 15–30% predictable new sales growth a year, tracked weekly
  • Works alongside any operating system already in place

Common questions

What people ask before we work together

Who is Sid Jashnani?

Sid Jashnani is a keynote speaker, the CEO of Outgrow and a London-based Outgrow Certified Advisor. He helps mid-market leadership teams install proactive selling as a daily habit, with a target of 15–30% predictable sales growth every year. He works across the US, UK and India, is a YPO member, and has been a founder since 2005. Outgrow itself was founded by Alex Goldfayn; Sid is its CEO and helps Alex execute the vision.

What does Sid speak on?

Sid delivers one keynote: Outgrow: How to Expand Market Share and Outsell Your Competition. It shows a room where their next block of revenue is sitting and the daily habits that bring it in. He speaks at conferences, industry events, PE portfolio days, leadership offsites and in the media, across the US, UK, India and internationally.

What is the Outgrow Workshop?

The Outgrow Workshop is a full day with your sales managers and salespeople. The team learns how Outgrow works from start to finish, sees why doing the work matters more than knowing about it, and practises the habits that bring in new sales on real accounts. Everyone leaves with tools they can use the next morning. Sid runs it in person across the US, UK and India, or online.

What is the Outgrow methodology?

Outgrow is a revenue growth methodology that installs simple, proactive customer communication behaviours into a business. It replaces reactive, hope-based selling with repeatable daily disciplines, aiming for 15–30% predictable annual revenue growth. The focus is on existing relationships, not new lead generation. Sales doing, not sales training.

Who does Sid work with?

Mid-market businesses generating $50M–$500M in annual revenue across Technology, SaaS, Professional Services, Insurance, Distribution, Manufacturing and Healthcare, primarily in the US, UK and India. Outgrow works best when the product is proven and the growth constraint is behaviour, not product-market fit.
