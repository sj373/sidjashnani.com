Sid Jashnani is the CEO of Outgrow and a London-based Certified Outgrow Advisor. He helps leadership teams install proactive selling as a daily habit and compound it into 15–30% predictable sales growth, every year. Sales doing, not sales training.

Sid has been a founder since 2005. He scaled a systems integration business into a £27M portfolio across the IT infrastructure ecosystem. Along the way he lived the inconsistent quarters, the delegation gaps, the cash-flow pressure, and the daily firefighting most founders know too well.

Then he came across Outgrow — Alex Goldfayn's methodology. The insight was straightforward: operational excellence alone doesn't drive growth. Proactive communication does. Reaching out to customers and prospects when nothing is wrong. Sid installed the habit inside his own teams, watched it compound, and became a Certified Advisor.

Today Sid is CEO of Outgrow, the company Alex Goldfayn founded, where he helps Alex execute the vision. His role is to bring clarity, consistency and commercial discipline to the business, and to support Outgrow's advisors and clients across the world. He also works directly with growth-stage leadership teams across the US, UK and India. The work is simple: install the proactive habit, track it weekly, and let it compound. Year on year. On purpose.

05 Common questions

What leaders ask before we work together

Direct answers to the questions that come up most. On Outgrow, on how we work, and on what predictable sales growth actually looks like in practice.

Who is Sid Jashnani?+
Sid Jashnani is the CEO of Outgrow and a London-based Certified Outgrow Advisor. He helps growth-stage leadership teams install proactive selling as a daily habit and compound it into 15–30% predictable sales growth, every year. He works across the US, UK and India, is a YPO member, and has been a founder since 2005. Outgrow itself was founded by Alex Goldfayn; Sid is its CEO and helps Alex execute the vision.
What is the Outgrow methodology?+
Outgrow is a revenue growth methodology that installs simple, proactive customer communication behaviours into a business. It replaces reactive, hope-based selling with repeatable daily disciplines that compound into 15–30% predictable annual revenue growth. The focus is on existing relationships, not new lead generation. Sales doing, not sales training.
What does a Certified Outgrow Advisor do?+
A Certified Outgrow Advisor installs the Outgrow revenue growth system inside a business. This involves diagnosing the growth constraint, installing proactive selling disciplines, creating accountability cadences, and tracking progress toward 15–30% annual revenue growth. Advisors are certified by Outgrow at runoutgrow.com.
What does predictable revenue growth mean in practice?+
Predictable revenue growth means replacing sporadic, reactive sales activity with repeatable daily disciplines. Using Outgrow, businesses install proactive outreach habits that generate consistent results (typically 15–30% annual revenue growth) independent of the founder's personal network or any single salesperson.
What size companies does Sid Jashnani work with?+
Growth-stage businesses generating £2M–£150M in annual revenue across Technology, SaaS, Professional Services, Insurance, Distribution, Manufacturing and Healthcare. Primarily in the US, UK and India.
How do I know if my business is ready for Outgrow?+
You are likely ready if: revenue growth has become inconsistent or over-dependent on the founder; your team is reactive rather than proactively engaging customers; or sales feels like luck rather than a system. Outgrow works best when the product is proven and the growth constraint is behaviour, not product-market fit.

Sid Jashnani is based in London, United Kingdom. He works with businesses across the US, UK and India, and is available internationally for speaking engagements and advisory work.
