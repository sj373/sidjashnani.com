Outgrow Advisory in the UK: proactive selling, tracked every week

If your business does good work for its customers but sales have flattened, or they only really move when the founder picks up the phone, Outgrow Advisory was designed with you in mind. It's a structured engagement in which Sid Jashnani, CEO of Outgrow and Outgrow Certified Advisor, works with your leadership team to put Alex Goldfayn's Outgrow system to work inside your business. Your customer-facing people make a handful of proactive communications a day, which takes just a few minutes, and you and Sid look at those efforts together every week.

Who Outgrow Advisory is for

Sid works with mid-market businesses generating $50M–$500M in annual revenue, mainly in Technology, SaaS, Professional Services, Insurance, Distribution, Manufacturing and Healthcare. He is based in London and works with leadership teams across the UK, the US and India. The approach works best when your product or service is already proven and the thing holding growth back is behaviour, meaning how your people go to customers, rather than a question of product-market fit.

Most of the leaders who get in touch recognise the same picture. The team is busy all day, but almost all of that activity is reactive. Customers ring with a problem, someone solves it, and the next call comes in. Alex describes this in Outgrow as the default for most business-to-business companies, and he points out that when customers only hear from their suppliers when something has gone wrong, the first thing they think when a salesperson calls is "What's wrong?" That's the habit Outgrow Advisory sets out to change.

What proactive selling means in practice

Alex defines proactive selling as "communicating with customers and prospects when they aren't expecting you (unscheduled), and when nothing is wrong." In practice that means short, friendly calls and messages to customers your team hasn't spoken to for a while, asking what else they have coming up that you could help with, mentioning another product or service they might not know you offer, following up on quotes, and asking happy customers for referrals. Each of these takes seconds or a few minutes, which matters because your people are already stretched and nobody can add an hour a day to their workload.

How the engagement works

The work starts with an honest look at where your growth is stuck. Sid helps you work out whether the revenue gap comes from behaviour, such as reactive selling or a heavy dependency on the founder, or whether something about the product or market needs attention first. There's little point building a sales habit on top of a product problem, so this step comes before anything else.

Once that's clear, the focus moves to building the proactive habit. Your team learns the Outgrow communications and starts making a few of them every day, mostly with existing customers and warm relationships, because that's where the quickest and most natural growth tends to sit.

The third part is a simple weekly rhythm. In the book, Alex describes a short weekly huddle run by managers, where the team looks at the numbers, shares the best wins from the week and agrees who to get in touch with next. Sid helps your managers set that up so it fits the way your business already runs, with light tracking that doesn't turn into admin.

Finally, the efforts are tracked week by week alongside the revenue they lead to. Outgrow tracks swings, not hits, which means your people are measured on the proactive efforts they make rather than on whether each one turns into an order. They can control their swings, and as the swings add up, the hits follow.

Why mindset gets so much attention

Alex is clear that behaviour follows thinking, and that it's impossible for salespeople to outsell their mindset. Plenty of good salespeople hold back from calling a customer because they're worried about bothering them or being turned down. Telling them to make three calls a day doesn't remove that worry, which is why so many of those instructions quietly fade. Outgrow starts by helping people see that they're helping their customers, not selling to them. Once they believe that, they pick up the phone with much more confidence, and the daily work stops feeling like a chore.

Why outside support helps the habit last

Many owners, quite reasonably, feel they could run something like this themselves. Sid has written in PREDICTABLE about why that so rarely happens. The person asked to hold everyone to the daily calls is usually the same person who sits next to them at lunch, so when a busy week gets in the way, it's easy to let it slide, and within a couple of months the calls have become optional again. Working with an Outgrow Certified Advisor gives the habit someone outside the day-to-day who keeps looking at the numbers with you every week.

What you're working towards

The aim is for your team to reach out to customers and prospects when nothing is wrong as a normal part of every day, so that revenue no longer depends on the founder or on any one seller. You'll be working towards a target of 15–30% predictable new sales growth a year, tracked weekly. Outgrow Advisory also works alongside whatever operating system you already have in place, so you don't need to replace your CRM, your meetings or your management structure to begin.

FAQs

Who is Outgrow Advisory for?

It's for mid-market businesses generating $50M–$500M in annual revenue, across sectors such as Technology, SaaS, Professional Services, Insurance, Distribution, Manufacturing and Healthcare, mainly in the UK, the US and India. It suits companies whose product is proven and whose growth is being held back by how the team goes to customers.

How much time will it take my team each day?

Very little. Alex built Outgrow around communications that take seconds or a few minutes each, so your customer-facing people can fit a handful of them into an ordinary working day alongside everything else they do.

Do we have to change our CRM or the way we run the business?

No. Outgrow Advisory works alongside any operating system you already have in place. The proactive work sits on top of what your team does today, and the tracking can be kept simple.

Is 15–30% growth a promise?

No. It's the target the work is aimed at, and progress is tracked every week. Results depend on how consistently your team makes its proactive efforts, which is exactly why those efforts are what gets measured.
