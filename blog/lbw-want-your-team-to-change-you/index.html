LBW: Want your team to change? You first.

Sid Jashnani, CEO of Outgrow and Outgrow Certified Advisor

The embarrassing lesson I learned about walking the talk.

Originally published on Substack (opens in a new tab). Subscribe (opens in a new tab) for the next letter.

TLDR: Your company initiatives are failing because you (yes, YOU) aren’t following them. Ask me how I know.

2015: I went full Tony Robbins.

  • Dropped $$$ on leadership programs and exec education

  • Filled notebooks with “game-changing” frameworks

  • Bought enough business books to start a library

  • Almost got a “hustle harder” tattoo (kidding... maybe)

My company, Momentum, was scaling fast. $4M → $35M.

But here’s the thing about scaling...

It’s like trying to build a plane while flying it. Except I was the pilot telling everyone else to follow the manual while freestyling my own moves

The Leadership Trap

Quick story time:

Me in meetings: “Guys, we NEED systems and processes!” Also me: hasn’t updated my scorecard in 3 weeks

Me to team: “No phones in Level 10 meetings!” Also me: sending emails under the table like a high schooler texting in math class

Me on processes: “Follow the system!” Also me: “But this deal is different...”

Notice a pattern?

Rules Don’t Apply To Me

Know what happens when the founder plays by different rules?

  • Trust dies faster than my New Year’s resolutions

  • Eye rolls become an Olympic sport

  • “Yes boss” becomes code for “whatever you say, buddy”

  • New initiatives are treated like crypto in 2024

The Numbers Don’t Lie

In departments where we actually followed the system:

  • Project completion: ⬆️ 40%

  • Chaos: ⬇️ 80%

  • Hair pulling: ⬇️ 90%

But because I kept making “founder exceptions,” we couldn’t scale it.

The Wake-Up Call

Every Monday at 10am, L10 time. The rules were crystal clear:

  • No phones

  • To-dos completed

  • Scorecard updated

  • Issues list prepped

Me at 10:07am, typing on my phone: “Sorry team, client emergency. Keep going, I’m listening...”

Until one Monday my Integrator just stood up: “Let’s cancel today. We’ll restart when you’re ready to play by your own rules.”

The Fix

Want your company to actually change?

  • Do the thing

  • Keep doing the thing

  • No “special founder circumstances”

  • Seriously, just keep doing the thing

That’s it. That’s the newsletter.

When I finally started eating my own dog food

  • Meetings got shorter

  • Decisions got faster

  • Problems got solved

  • My blood pressure dropped

The Bottom Line

Stop being the “rules for thee but not for me” founder. (Trust me, your team already has a group chat about it)

Keep it real,

Sid

P.S. I still have those 2015 notebooks. They’re great for propping up my monitor. At least they’re supporting something.

Know a leader who preaches process but plays by different rules? Forward this email. Their team already has a group chat about it.
