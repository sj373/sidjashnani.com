How to Build Predictable Sales Without Carrying the Number

Sid Jashnani, CEO of Outgrow and Outgrow Certified Advisor

A proven way to grow revenue without founder heroics

Originally published on Substack (opens in a new tab). Subscribe (opens in a new tab) for the next letter.

A founder said something to me recently that I cannot shake.

“Take me out of the equation, and sales will collapse.”

He was not proud of it.
He was exhausted by it.

And I have heard versions of this sentence for seven years as a business coach. Different words. Same problem.

These were not broken companies.

Clear vision.
Good people in the right seats.
Strong accountability.
Solid processes.

And yet sales still felt fragile. Personal. Founder dependent.

That question kept bothering me.

Why do some companies grow predictably, while others struggle every quarter?

The pattern I kept seeing

The struggling companies were not lazy or incompetent.

They simply did not have a system for sales. They were banking on founder-led sales and their relationships or were waiting for their phone to ring.

They had systems for operations.
Systems for finance.
Systems for hiring.

But revenue was reactive.

Waiting for inbound leads.
Hoping the pipeline fills.
Leaning on the founder’s relationships to close deals.

Hope is not a strategy. I learned that painfully in my first two businesses.

The companies that grew consistently did one thing differently.

They took swings (actions) every single day.
Not when things were desperate.
Not at quarter end.

Daily. Relentless. Boring. Effective.

Why I became an Outgrow Certified Advisor (opens in a new tab)

I believe deeply in every company implementing a business operating system. It gives companies clarity, structure, discipline, and execution.

But a business operating system does not fully solve sales.

It creates the structure around the business, accountabilities and processes.
Revenue growth needs its own operating system.

That is where Outgrow fits.

Outgrow has been around for over 20 years under different names.
More than 500 companies run on it.

The ones that commit to the work average 15–30% annual revenue growth, year after year, as long as they run the system.

That is not a promise.
That is a track record.

What makes Outgrow different

1. Sales is a habit, not a department.
Most companies outsource growth to the sales team or the founder.
Outgrow involves every customer facing role.
Everyone who touches a customer can create opportunity (or kill it).

2. It is about doing, not training
I have sat through enough sales workshops that spike energy for a week and disappear.
Outgrow is daily action.
Choreographed work.
Consistent swings.

3. It creates positivity, not pressure
No fear tactics.
No manipulation.
Just habits that feel good and produce results.
People want to do the work because they see it working.

The math is simple

More swings (actions) equals more hits (revenue).

You cannot control who says yes.
You can control how many conversations you start.
How many follow ups you make.
How many relationships you nurture.

Control the controllables.
Let the results follow.

That is the missing piece I was looking for.

What this means for my work

I am still a coach helping you implement the world’s best business operating system. That does not change.

But now I can offer something I could not before.
A proven way to build predictable revenue without the founder carrying the load.

If your sales feel inconsistent
If you are tired of hoping next quarter will be different
If you have ever thought “take me out and this falls apart”

I would love to talk.

No pitch.
Just a real conversation about what is working and what is not.
