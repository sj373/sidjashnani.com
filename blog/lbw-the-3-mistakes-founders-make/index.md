Most founders make these and they’re easy to fix.

Hiring offshore can save you 60–70% in costs. But most founders still struggle to get results. Why? Because they unknowingly make the same three mistakes over and over again.

---

## Mistake #1: Treating Them Like Outsiders

You can’t build trust with distance.

Too many leaders treat their offshore staff as “vendors” or “assistants” instead of teammates.

The fix is simple:

- Welcome them like you would an in-office hire.
- Introduce them to the team on Day 1.
- Share your story, your culture, your rituals.

Belonging drives performance. Not payroll.

---

## Mistake #2: Dumping Tasks Without Context

I see this all the time. An offshore team member logs in for the first time and within 20 minutes, they’re handed a monster task.

They’re nervous. They don’t know your culture or expectations yet. They just joined.

Ease them in. Start with simple wins that build confidence and rhythm. Then scale responsibility as trust builds.

---

## Mistake #3: Ghosting on Communication

No meetings. No check-ins. No feedback. Just silence and Slack pings.

Communication isn’t optional, it’s the oxygen of remote teams. Start with:

- A **daily 10-minute huddle** (even async updates work)
- A **weekly one-on-one** to align expectations and coach performance
- Make them part of the correct departmental or appropriate Level 10 meeting.

Your offshore people don’t need micromanagement. They need presence.

---

## The Bottom Line

Treat them like insiders. Onboard them properly. Communicate like clockwork.

Do this, and your offshore team will perform as if they’re right across the hall not across the ocean.
