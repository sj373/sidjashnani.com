Why great leaders manage inputs, not outcomes

I teach leaders how to make faster, clearer decisions.

And yet, for years, I relied on my gut and got it wrong about half the time.

High stakes decisions. Big consequences. Made on intuition. Ego. Experience.

A coin toss.

Sometimes it worked. Sometimes it blew up.

That is not strategy. That is gambling.

---

## The Realisation

Intuition is not the problem.

Unfiltered intuition is.

Most founders overtrust their gut because it feels decisive. Confident. Leader like.

But confidence without data is just guesswork with better posture.

So I learned to add data behind the madness.

---

## The Shift That Changed Everything

For every business or division, I now run a weekly scorecard and monthly P&L review.

Not a vanity dashboard. Not lagging results.

A simple weekly scorecard focused on **inputs**.

Not revenue this month But sales calls made.

Not new clients won But proposals sent.

Not utilisation achieved But client meetings held.

These are the only things I can truly control.

---

## Why Inputs Matter More Than Outputs

Outputs are history. Inputs are leverage.

If you obsess over outcomes, you react. If you manage inputs, you lead.

When inputs are strong and consistent, results usually follow. When results are weak, the scorecard tells me exactly where the breakdown is.

No emotion. No ego. No storytelling.

Just signal.

---

## Intuition Still Has a Seat at the Table

I have not abandoned instinct.

I have disciplined it.

Intuition is a hypothesis. The scorecard is the test.

Together, they give me clarity on three decisions every week:

- Go
- No go
- Pivot

That is how you raise your batting average as a leader.

---

## The Leadership Question

Most leaders are obsessing over outputs they cannot directly control.

While ignoring the inputs that actually drive them.

So I will leave you with this:

**What inputs are you ignoring while obsessing over outcomes?**

Because if you do not manage the inputs, the business will manage you.
