# Referrals aren’t luck

- Mirror: https://sidjashnani.com/blog/referrals-arent-luck/

- Author: Sid Jashnani, CEO of Outgrow and Outgrow Certified Advisor
- Type: Letter

Stop waiting for referrals. Start asking for them.

**Referrals aren’t luck. They’re a question nobody asks.**

Every founder loves a referral.

Warmest lead there is. Half sold before the first call. The cheapest pipeline in the business.

And almost nobody asks for them on purpose.

We treat referrals like weather. Something that happens to us. A nice surprise when it lands, a quiet disappointment when it doesn’t.

Here is the truth. Your happiest customer right now knows two or three people who should be buying what you sell. People in their world. People with the same problem you just solved for them.

They would introduce you gladly.

You have just never asked.

## Why we don’t ask.

It feels needy.

You did good work, the client is happy, and something in you says don’t push it. Don’t spoil it. Don’t look like you need it.

That is the same voice that kills proactive selling everywhere. The fear of being a bother.

So flip it round.

You are not asking for a favour. You are giving someone you have helped the chance to help someone they care about. You solved a real problem for them. There is a person in their network with that exact problem, losing sleep over it right now. The introduction is a gift in both directions.

See it that way and the ask stops feeling like begging.

## The ask, made specific.

“Do you know anyone who might need me?” gets you nothing. It is too big. The brain goes blank.

Make it small and specific.

“Who else do you know who is dealing with the exact thing I just fixed for you?”

“You said you are happy with how this went. Is there one person you would feel comfortable introducing me to?”

One problem. One person. Easy to answer.

## Why it has to be systematic.

One referral ask is easy. Remembering to ask, every time, across everyone who touches a customer, is hard. That is where it falls apart. It gets left to the moments people happen to feel brave.

So you stop leaving it to chance.

In Outgrow, the referral ask is a prescribed action. Scripted, assigned, logged, and reviewed every week alongside the proactive calls and the “did you know” questions. It gets counted like every other swing, because we know the batting average. Ask enough happy customers, and a predictable share say yes.

Luck becomes a number you can grow.

## The shift.

Stop hoping your best customers send people your way.

Start asking them, on purpose, every week.

Who is the one happy customer you could ask this week, and whose name do you think they would give you?
